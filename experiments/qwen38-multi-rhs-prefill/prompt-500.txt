Write three paragraphs of magical-realist prose inspired by the following scenes.

The cartographer Marguerite Pell discovery that every coastline on her atlas had shifted three miles inland overnight, and the tide tables of every port she had memorized as an apprentice were now wrong by exactly the same amount. The harbor master of a small fishing town refused to update his charts, arguing that the sea had no right to renegotiate a boundary that had held since his grandmother kept the lighthouse. A courier arrives carrying a second atlas, printed in a country that does not exist, in which every coast matches the new shoreline perfectly and includes annotations for harbors that have not been built yet.

Meanwhile, in the engine house at the edge of the marsh, the machinist Ovid Terhune found that his lathe had begun cutting threads that matched no standard ever published, and the bolts it produced fit only the old bridge across the canal, which had been demolished a decade earlier to make room for a pump station that was never built. The borough engineer confiscated four of the bolts and mailed them to the capital; the postal service returned the parcel unopened, stamped DELIVERED TO SENDER IN 1911. Ovid kept working, and by Thursday the scrap bin had organized itself into a scale model of the canal district as it would look after a flood that the weather service insists will never arrive.

At the night market on Calibration Street, a vendor sold pairs of mismatched gloves: every left glove had belonged to someone who was later forgotton by everyone who ever met them, and the right gloves were new. The price of a pair depended on the number of letters in the buyer's childhood street, rounded up to the nearest even number, and haggling was permitted only in languages the vendor did not speak. A student bought a pair and found that wearing them made sheet music legible again, though only for pieces that had been out of print since before the war, and only in rooms where a clock was running exactly four minutes fast. She began to inventory every clock in the city, and the inventory itself began to inventory her.
